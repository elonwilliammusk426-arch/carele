# Apex Motor Group: Personal Sourcing, Proxy Shopping & Consignment Service
## Comprehensive Business & Operational Plan

---

### 1. Executive Summary
**Apex Motor Group** is a boutique automotive brokerage specializing in bespoke personal vehicle sourcing (proxy shopping) and white-glove vehicle consignment. We bridge the gap between discerning collectors, enthusiasts, and high-net-worth buyers by providing end-to-end transparency, rigorous verification, global logistics management, and secure transaction escrow for rare, exotic, classic, and enthusiast automobiles.

---

### 2. Core Service Pillars

#### A. Personal Sourcing & Proxy Shopping ("The Hunt")
* **Target Audience:** Enthusiasts and collectors seeking hard-to-find, allocation-locked, discontinued, or international vehicles (e.g., Porsche GT cars, vintage air-cooled 911s, JDM legends, European imports).
* **The Process:**
  1. **Consultation & Brief:** Define exact specifications (year, mileage, colorway, provenance, budget max).
  2. **Global Search & Vetting:** Leverage private dealer networks, auction houses (Bring a Trailer, RM Sotheby's, Collecting Cars), and private collections worldwide.
  3. **Proxy Inspection:** Deploy certified master technicians for compression tests, paint meter analysis, undercarriage inspection, and DME report generation.
  4. **Acquisition & Import:** Handle bidding, negotiation, currency exchange, customs clearance, and enclosed trailer transport to the client's garage.

#### B. White-Glove Consignment ("The Showcase")
* **Target Audience:** Owners looking to sell high-value automobiles without the friction, lowball offers, and security risks of public marketplaces.
* **The Process:**
  1. **Valuation & Prep:** Market data analysis, professional detailing, paint correction, and studio photography/videography.
  2. **Storage & Security:** Climate-controlled, insured storage facility with 24/7 biometric security and trickle chargers.
  3. **Targeted Marketing:** Private syndication to vetted buyers, digital campaigns, and curated listings.
  4. **Transaction & Escrow:** Secure escrow services, title transfer management, and seamless payout.

---

### 3. Revenue & Fee Structure

* **Consignment Commission:**
  * Standard tier (vehicles under $150k): 7% of final sale price.
  * Elite/Exotic tier (vehicles $150k - $500k): 5% of final sale price.
  * Hypercar tier (vehicles $500k+): Negotiable (typically 3–4%).
  * *Includes professional media production, climate storage, and platform listing.*

* **Personal Sourcing / Proxy Shopping Fees:**
  * **Retainer Fee:** $2,500 upfront (crediting toward final purchase fee) to initiate global search and inspection dispatch.
  * **Success Fee:** 3% to 5% of purchase price (or flat fee agreed upon contractually based on vehicle rarity and import complexity).
  * **Inspection Add-on:** Fixed rate per independent PPI (Pre-Purchase Inspection) + travel expenses if international.

---

### 4. Operational Workflow & Risk Management

* **Escrow & Title Security:** All funds are held in bonded escrow accounts until clean title verification and physical delivery inspection are completed.
* **Authentication & Provenance:** Every vehicle undergoes a rigorous 150-point inspection verifying VIN matching, factory options (build sheets), accident history (Carfax/AutoCheck/HPI), and service records.
* **Logistics Partnerships:** Exclusive partnerships with nationwide and international enclosed transport carriers with full-value transit insurance.

---

### 5. Go-To-Market Strategy
* **Private Networking:** Concierge partnerships with luxury real estate agents, private wealth managers, and exotic car clubs.
* **Digital Presence:** High-aesthetic web platform featuring cinematic gallery showcases, transparent sourcing requests, and live inventory.
* **Content Marketing:** Documenting rare finds, restoration projects, and market trends via YouTube, Instagram, and curated newsletters.
