Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does the sourcing retainer work? ↓
We require an upfront search retainer of $2,000 to initiate global search protocols, deploy inspectors, and verify off-market Tesla allocations. This retainer credits directly toward our final acquisition success fee upon purchase.
Where are consigned Teslas stored? ↓
All consigned electric vehicles and home energy units are kept in our climate-controlled vault facilities equipped with 24/7 trickle charging, individual perimeter security, and full-value garagekeepers insurance.
What are your consignment commission rates? ↓
Consignment commissions are tiered based on vehicle valuation: 6% for vehicles under $60k, 5% for models between $60k and $150k, and negotiable (4%) for high-end Roadsters or Cybertruck editions. This includes all studio cinematography and storage.
How do you handle software account transfers and FSD? ↓
Our concierge handles complete Tesla account migration, Full Self-Driving (Supervised) transfer verification, and Supercharging credit adjustments directly through Tesla corporate channels upon closing.
